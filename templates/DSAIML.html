Course Overview
The Complete Data Intelligence Stack

Data Science Foundation

NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib — collect, clean, analyze, and visualize data to drive business decisions.

Machine Learning

Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning. Build models that predict, classify, and cluster.
